Included input styles: Thin, ExtraLight, Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold.
Only supplemental symbol outlines are incorporated: selected arrows, mathematical
and technical symbols, box drawing, blocks, geometric shapes, and Powerline symbols.
No JetBrains Mono Latin letters or programming ligatures are incorporated.

Rajdhani Mono modifications
--------------------------
Copyright 2026 Rajdhani Mono contributors.
The derivative changes character advances, positions and selected outlines;
redesigns I/l/1/0 and underscores; adds combining-accent positioning and Romanian
T-comma-below; adapts terminal symbols; renames the family; and regenerates hints.
It is built from the pinned binary inputs with fontTools and ttfautohint.

The upstream names are used to identify source materials and acknowledge authors.
Indian Type Foundry and JetBrains do not endorse this unofficial project.

Build tools are external dependencies with their own licenses. They are not
redistributed as part of this repository. Requirements are pinned in requirements.txt.
Source input checksums are listed in sources/manifest.json.
